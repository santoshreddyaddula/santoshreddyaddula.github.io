Biography

Dr. Santosh Reddy Addula is a Senior Member of IEEE and a research scholar at UC. He holds a Ph.D. and a Master of Science degree in Information Technology, bringing extensive experience across multiple domains in the IT industry.

His research focuses on smart technologies and sustainable innovation. He has published numerous papers in reputable Scopus-indexed journals and conferences and has served as a reviewer for international journals and conferences. Dr. Addula currently serves as an Associate Editor and Editorial Board Member for journals published by IGI Global and Sage Journal.
